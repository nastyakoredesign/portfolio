TL;DR

One redesigned portal for four brands and a client app on iOS and Android

  1. A UK portal where financial advisers manage clients and their clients see finances and documents. Three firms run it under their own brands.
  2. My part: redesign and new vision of most features, the client app MVP, a design system with tokens per brand, and review of every module the developers built.
  3. The app is live on iOS and Android. 30 of 61 portal sections already run on the new design.

Context and problem

Financial advisers in the UK use Centology to run their whole client relationship: clients, cases, forms, documents and messages. Their private clients log in to see plans, assets, wills and attitude to risk. Three other firms run the same portal under their own brands.

When I joined, the portal looked dated and every section worked its own way. Screens were overloaded, roles were easy to get lost in, and grid columns shrank until you couldn't read them. White labels were recolored by hand. My audit and Centology's brief named the same problems.

An earlier redesign concept hadn't gone ahead, so the task was to modernize the portal without stopping a live product.

Constraints

  • One design system for four brands: Centology and three white labels.
  • A live product: new screens shipped in stages next to old ones.
  • Backend limits on some features, solved through compromises with the developers.

My role

What I did

  • As the only designer on a 13-person team, I redesigned most portal features and reworked their logic with the BA.
  • Designed the client app MVP: 25 flows, now on iOS and Android.
  • Built the design system for four brands, with guidelines written for developers and AI agents.
  • Designed AI features: a prompt manager for admins and AI plan summaries for clients and advisers.
  • Reviewed every module the developers built against the design and spec, for every role.

UX research

Before redesigning, I walked through the old portal's key flows for each role, compared how sections handled the same tasks, and checked my findings against Centology's brief. Below is one flow: an adviser creating a case.

How might we…

  • …let advisers create a case without leaving the list?Case Hub
  • …show the whole menu to each role at once?Navigation
  • …make every section add and edit things the same way?Records

Process

I broke each request down with the BA and drew the screens first. The BA then finished the spec based on them, and Centology reviewed every version.

Solutions

Web portal

Shipped

Case Hub: a list you can scan at a glance

Why this way Each case is a card with what an adviser checks first: client, stage, due date, priority and status. Search, filters, sort and totals sit above, so the next case is found without opening any. A table view is one switch away when you need it.

Logic from specs States · Roles · Edge cases
States
Tiles or a table, and the choice holds until logout. Totals and the chart follow filters and search.
Roles
Admins, adviser admins and advisers create, view and edit cases. An admin can switch to an adviser's view, but every change is still recorded under the admin's own account.
Edge cases
Add Case opens the side panel. Fields that depend on others stay locked with a hint on what to fill first, documents unlock after the first save, and closing with unsaved changes asks first.
Shipped

Plans: totals first, one plan at a time

Why this way Clients see totals first and open one plan at a time. An AI summary, tuned by admins in a prompt manager I designed, explains each policy in plain English for clients and their advisers.

Logic from specs States · Roles · Edge cases
States
Tiles by default, a table on demand; the choice holds until logout. Totals update with filters and search.
Roles
Clients see their plans read-only, without internal notes or edit buttons. Advisers see the same view when they sign in as the client.
Edge cases
AI Summary first shows a "not financial advice" disclaimer. If no prompt is switched on in the prompt manager, it says the feature is temporarily unavailable.
Concept

Corporate clients: a new client type, designed from scratch

Why this way Corporate clients go first: we shape the new client layout here, on a feature built from scratch, then carry it over to private clients.

Logic from specs States · Roles · Edge cases
States
New, edit and details modes in one panel; related tabs unlock after the first save.
Roles
It adds a new Employee client type. An adviser links an employee to the company in Connections and switches on Access; the company then shows up in that employee's profile.
Edge cases
Company details can come from Companies House; the form handles an imported company, no match, and a duplicate company number.

Mobile app (MVP)

Shipped

Client finances, documents and adviser chat in one app

Client app for private clients: plans, assets, incomes and expenditures, documents and secure chat with their adviser. Live on iOS and Android.

Why this way Centology chose the scope: finances, documents and chat. I brought them to the phone on the portal's design system, with mobile-only components where needed.

From design to code

Empeek's developers built the portal from my screens, design-system components with every state, and the BA's spec. After each module I checked the build against the design and spec for every role. Most mismatches were spacing, sizes and responsive layout.

  • Tokens

    --color-brand

    I built a color mode per brand for design. Developers use the same color scale, with each brand's own values.

  • Guidelines

    guidelines.md

    Font, color, icon and status rules for developers and an AI agent, checked against the developers' data keys.

  • Implementation review

    review/board

    16 modules since October 2025, checked against design and spec for every role.

Outcome

  • ~1,100

    mismatches caught in review since Oct 2025, across 16 modules

  • 35 of 61

    portal sections redesigned, 30 already live

  • iOS + Android

    client app MVP, ~120 screens and states

What changed in the user flow

  • Records

    Every record opens in the same side panel with tabs, instead of its own long page or an oversized popup.

  • Plans

    Clients see their plan totals first and open one plan at a time, with an AI summary in plain English.

  • Navigation

    Every section sits in the sidebar, and the page path always shows where you are.

Takeaways

  • I'd build on tokens and variants from day one and agree them with developers then, instead of migrating later.
  • I'd keep frame and section names clean from the start, so an AI agent can find the right frame.
